Privacy Policy — Hivewright

Effective date: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Hivewright (“the game”) handles. The game is published by BlairsLab (“we”, “us”). If you have a question, contact us at privacy@blairslab.co.nz.

Summary

  • The game does not collect, store on our systems, or share any personal information.
  • It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics.
  • It does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send information anywhere.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

So you can continue playing, the game saves on your device:

  • Game progress: your hive, resources, research, equipment, climb progress and story progress.
  • Settings, such as music and sound-effect volume.

This information stays in the game’s private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

2. Information we collect

None. The game does not request internet access, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other permission, and it contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Separately from the game, Google Play may send us anonymous crash and performance reports through its Android vitals service, depending on your device settings. These do not identify you.

3. Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because the game does not collect any.

5. Keeping and deleting your information

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes your progress permanently. We cannot recover it, because we never had a copy.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Australia’s Privacy Act 1988, New Zealand’s Privacy Act 2020, the EU and UK GDPR, and US state privacy laws, you may have rights over personal information about you. We hold none, but if you have any question or request, contact us at privacy@blairslab.co.nz.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles information (for example by adding optional ads or purchases), we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released, and note the change in the game’s update notes.
