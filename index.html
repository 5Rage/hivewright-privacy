Privacy Policy — Hivewright

Effective date: 2 October 2026 · Updated 2 October 2026 to cover optional in-app purchases

This policy explains what information the mobile game Hivewright (“the game”) handles, why, and who it is shared with. The game is published by BlairsLab (“we”, “us”). If you have a question, contact us at privacy@blairslab.co.nz.

Summary

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device. We do not run servers that receive your game data.
  • Video ads are optional: one only plays when you choose to watch it for an in-game bonus. When you do, Google AdMob receives device and ad information to show the ad.
  • Purchases are optional and handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

So you can continue playing, the game saves on your device:

  • Game progress: your hive, resources, research, equipment, climb progress and story progress.
  • Settings, such as music and sound-effect volume.
  • Records of earned ad rewards, so each bonus is given exactly once.
  • For purchases: a one-way fingerprint of each purchase, so it is given exactly once, and whether you own the Lantern Pass. The fingerprint cannot be turned back into your purchase details.

This information stays in the game’s private storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the game. The game does not request location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or similar permissions, and it contains no analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.

Google Play may send us anonymous crash and performance reports through its Android vitals service, depending on your device settings. These do not identify you.

3. Optional rewarded video ads (Google AdMob)

The game can offer optional video ads that give an in-game bonus. A video only plays when you choose to watch one; there are no forced ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, and are limited to content suitable for general audiences. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • device information, such as device model, operating system, language and screen size;
  • the device’s advertising identifier, where permitted;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • information about the ads shown and your interactions with them, and diagnostic information about ad performance.

This information is encrypted in transit. If you are in a region that requires it, such as the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, Google’s consent form asks whether ads may be personalised before any ad loads. How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).

4. Optional in-app purchases (Google Play)

The game offers optional purchases, such as the Lantern Pass (which removes video ads), time skips and resource bundles. Nothing is required to play. Purchases are made and paid for through Google Play billing, a service of Google LLC; we never receive your card or payment details. When you buy something, Google Play gives the game a signed receipt, which the game checks on your device before granting the item. The game also asks Google Play which permanent purchases you own, so it can restore them on a new device and remove them if they are refunded. This happens on your device; purchase information is not sent to us. Google’s handling of payments is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice. Google provides us with order records for our accounting, as it does for every app on Google Play.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help delete it.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

  • Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes your progress. We cannot recover it.
  • Information collected by Google AdMob is kept under Google’s policies. You can manage it through your Google account settings, and you can reset your advertising ID at any time.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Australia’s Privacy Act 1988, New Zealand’s Privacy Act 2020, the EU and UK GDPR, and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal information about you on our systems, most requests are best handled on your device or with Google directly, but we are happy to help. Contact us at privacy@blairslab.co.nz.

9. Security

Your game data is stored in the game’s private storage on your device. Ads and payments are handled by Google using its own security measures.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect. Significant changes will also be noted in the game’s update notes.
